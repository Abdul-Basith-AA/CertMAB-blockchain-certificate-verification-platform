Platform Features

Discover the core components that make CertMAB the most secure and reliable platform for certificate verification.

Immutable Blockchain Ledger

Every certificate hash is recorded on a distributed ledger, making it permanent and impossible to alter or delete, ensuring ultimate data integrity.

Decentralized Storage

Certificate metadata and encrypted files are stored on IPFS via Pinata, ensuring high availability and censorship resistance.

Smart Contract Automation

Verification processes and access rules are governed by self-executing smart contracts, reducing manual overhead and eliminating human error.

Instantaneous Verification

Employers can verify the authenticity of any certificate in seconds using its unique cryptographic hash, streamlining the hiring process.

Unified Dashboards

Dedicated portals for Admins, Institutions, Companies, and Students provide a seamless user experience with tailored functionalities.

Cross-Verification Engine

Our automated system periodically cross-verifies data between the blockchain and database, instantly flagging any discrepancies.

Enterprise-Grade Security

Our platform is built on a foundation of security, protecting user data and ensuring the integrity of every certificate.

End-to-End Encryption

All data, both in transit and at rest, is protected with AES-256 encryption, the same standard used by governments and financial institutions.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Strict access controls ensure that users can only view and perform actions permitted by their role, minimizing the risk of unauthorized access.

Continuous Security Audits

Our systems undergo regular security scans and periodic penetration testing to identify and patch vulnerabilities before they can be exploited.
